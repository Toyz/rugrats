# Database 22's animation files

`DB22FRM.BIN` (LBA 124897, 4544 bytes) and `DB22ROT.BIN` (LBA 124999, 18824
bytes) are on the disc, but the table's slots for them (328 and 360) are 0.
DB22's MOD, ANM, HIE, LP, LEVEL, COL, A.RAW and TEXPOS are all in the table.
